DUKA connects your DUKA ventilation units to Homey over your local network,
without a cloud service. Control fan speed, ventilation mode, boost, 24-hour
mode, interval ventilation and filter status from the device tile or Homey
Flows. The app finds your units during pairing. It requires Homey Pro or Homey
Self-Hosted Server with LAN access.

This is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with DUKA Ventilation.
